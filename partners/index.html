Straight answers

Who owns the client relationship?

You do. For Referral deals we deliver as GrowwStacks, but we keep you in the loop and never pitch your client anything outside the engagement without you. For Reseller deals, the client only deals with your brand.

How do you decide who gets credit for a client?

Deal registration. Register the client before we've been in touch with them, and the first partner to register wins. Accounts we've been talking to in the last 6 months aren't eligible. We confirm every registration in writing.

How long is my registration protected?

90 days for the deal to come together. Once the client signs, you're credited on their revenue for 12 months (Strategic: 24 months).

What is commission calculated on?

GrowwStacks fees the client has actually paid, excluding taxes and third-party costs such as software licences and AI usage. [Confirm]

When and how do I get paid?

Within 15 days of the client paying us, by bank transfer or Wise. You send us an invoice for your share. Applicable tax deductions (for example, TDS for India-based partners) are applied as required by law.

What happens if a client gets a refund?

We never ask you to return money. If a client is refunded, we deduct your share of that refund from your future payouts, pro rata.

Is there a fee or minimum to join?

No fee for any tier. Referral has no minimum. Reseller keeps its rate with at least one closed deal every 6 months. Strategic needs 3 deals or $50,000 in revenue per year.

What if I don't hit my tier's minimum?

We review tiers once a year and move you to the tier that fits. Anything you've already earned stays yours. [Confirm]

How does white-label delivery work?

Proposals, documents and client calls are in your name. Our team can join calls as part of your team. We sign an NDA with you and never contact your client directly.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need client trust and a sense of where the pain is. We handle scoping, technical pre-sales and delivery.

Is there a contract?

Yes. A short partner agreement, signed before your first registered deal. It covers payouts, attribution, confidentiality and non-solicitation, both ways.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. You can start with Referral and move up as your deal flow grows.
